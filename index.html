Stay Safe from Online Scams

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers pretend to be trusted organizations to steal sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, OTPs, and bank details.

How to Identify Phishing Emails

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Never click suspicious links.
  • Look for spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Don't open unexpected attachments.
  • Verify requests before sharing personal information.

How to Identify Fake Websites

  • Always check the URL carefully.
  • Look for HTTPS and the padlock icon.
  • Avoid websites with spelling mistakes in the domain name.
  • Do not enter passwords on suspicious websites.
  • Verify the website before making online payments.

Common Social Engineering Techniques

  • Email Phishing
  • Spear Phishing
  • Smishing (SMS Phishing)
  • Vishing (Voice Phishing)
  • Fake Login Pages

Real-Life Case Study

Sarah received an email claiming to be from her bank asking her to verify her account immediately. She clicked the link and entered her username, password, and OTP on a fake website. Within minutes, money was transferred from her account. This happened because she trusted a fraudulent email and website.

What Should You Do?

  • Do not click any links.
  • Do not download attachments.
  • Report the email as phishing.
  • Delete the email.
  • Change your password immediately if you clicked a suspicious link.

Security Tips

  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Verify website URLs before logging in.
  • Report phishing emails immediately.
